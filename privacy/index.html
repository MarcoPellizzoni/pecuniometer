Privacy policy

Pecuniometer processes expense information on your Android device. The released app has no Internet permission, no account system, no advertising and no analytics.

1. Who is responsible

Pecuniometer is developed and published by Marco Pellizzoni. For privacy questions or requests, email pecuniometer@gmail.com.

2. Information handled by the app

The app can process and store:

  • expense amounts, dates, categories, optional notes, merchant names and whether an expense was entered or captured;
  • budget amounts, monthly cycle, alert settings, language, consent choices and merchant categorisation rules;
  • the names and Android package identifiers of user-installed apps, when you ask to choose a payment app;
  • the package identifier, title, body and posting time of notifications from payment apps you explicitly select, when automatic capture is enabled;
  • budget totals and labels needed by the optional home-screen widget and budget alerts.

Notification access and installed-app visibility are optional. Each is explained in the app before Android is asked for access. Pecuniometer does not inspect another app’s private storage.

3. Local processing, storage and sharing

Expense recognition and all app features run locally. Data is kept in Pecuniometer’s Android app-private storage and is not sent to Marco Pellizzoni, advertisers, analytics providers or another remote service. The release does not request Android’s Internet permission. Flet and Flutter provide the user interface; Pecuniometer does not use them as analytics or remote data-processing services.

Android backup or device-transfer features may copy private app data under your device, manufacturer and Google account settings. Those are Android system operations rather than network transmissions initiated by Pecuniometer. The optional widget exposes budget summaries to the Android launcher, and enabled alerts expose their displayed text to Android’s notification system.

4. Local diagnostics

Android system logs produced while the app operates can temporarily contain package identifiers and, for a recognised payment, its amount or merchant. A notification rejected by the local recognition rules can also appear in diagnostic logging. Pecuniometer does not upload these logs. Access to system logs is controlled by Android and device debugging tools. Diagnostic-only builds used during development are not the Google Play release.

5. Retention and deletion on the device

Expenses and settings remain until you change or remove them. You can edit or delete individual expenses in the app. At most 500 notifications awaiting local processing are queued; they are removed after a successful processing pass and the oldest are displaced when the limit is reached. The installed-app catalogue remains until an authorised refresh replaces it.

Use Android’s “Clear storage” control or uninstall Pecuniometer to remove all local app data. A later Android backup restoration can restore a copy. Because Pecuniometer has no user account or developer server, there is no server-side app account or expense copy for the developer to delete.

6. This website

These legal pages use no developer-added cookies, advertising or analytics. They are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and may process technical information under its own privacy statement. Visiting this website is separate from using the offline app.

7. Support email

If you contact the address above, the developer receives the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message and attachments. It is used only to answer the request, provide support, prevent abuse and keep records where legally necessary. Gmail is used to receive email and processes it under Google’s privacy policy. Support correspondence is deleted within 12 months after the request is resolved, unless it must be retained longer for a legal obligation or an ongoing dispute.

8. Legal bases and your rights

Where EU data-protection law applies to support correspondence, processing is necessary to answer your request or perform the related contract, for the legitimate interests of support and security, or to comply with law. You may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability where applicable, and may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also complain to your competent supervisory authority; in Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. The developer cannot access data that remains only on your device.

9. Children, security and changes

Pecuniometer is a general-audience budgeting utility and is not designed for children. Android app isolation, permission controls and affirmative disclosures protect local information, but no technical measure provides absolute security. This page will be updated before materially different data practices are released; the effective date identifies its version.
